# Provenance and trust

Layers on every public opportunity:

1. **Listing facts** — title, company, pay (if sourced), remote, location,
   country eligibility, skills/domain, source, verification freshness, apply URL
2. **Happy Tasking community intelligence** — TaskScore, payment reliability,
   project stability, TaskPulse, screening, issues. Real evidence only.
   Demo reports never attach to real jobs.
3. **Your TaskMatch** — logged-in estimated fit only. Not hiring probability.
   Anonymous visitors get a CTA, never a fake score.

## Discovery vs primary source

AITraining.jobs may discover a Mercor role. Mercor (or Greenhouse/xAI, etc.)
is the primary application destination when the apply URL points there.

Do not label AITraining.jobs as “Official public listing” unless it is the
first-party posting. Prefer:

- Official public listing
- Discovered through AITraining.jobs (internal/supporting note)

## Open job ≠ TaskPulse

An open opportunity means the company appears to be recruiting.
TaskPulse is contributor-reported task availability. Both can disagree.

## Missing data

Missing pay ≠ $0. Missing country ≠ worldwide. Remote ≠ worldwide.
`firstSeenAt` is Happy Tasking’s first ingest. `publishedAt` is the source
posted time when present. Missing TaskScore/TaskPulse is omitted.

## Commercial independence

Referral relationships never affect TaskScore, TaskPulse, payment reliability,
project stability, screening, issues, reviews, TaskMatch, or ranking.
