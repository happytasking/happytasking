## How this list was ranked (INTERNAL STRATEGIC ASSESSMENT)

1. Generic `ai training jobs` (**50,000**, +900%) is the largest clean generic CSV demand → TaskMatch.  
2. DataAnnotation brand cluster matches that scale (**50,000** jobs) on **one company page**.  
3. Mercor is a clean brand cluster at **5,000** jobs + **5,000** reviews.  
4–7. Role/remote/coding/evaluator jobs stay on TaskMatch (cluster, not new URLs).  
8. Only Guide with both SERP + CSV (**500**) as educational primary.  
9. Outlier is strategically important; CSV volume is contaminated.  
10. Comparisons: right URL type; wait for eligibility.

Not ranked #1 despite 50,000: `dataannotation jobs` is **company-specific**, not a second TaskMatch.
