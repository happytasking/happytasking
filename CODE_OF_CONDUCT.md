# Code of Conduct

Happy Tasking is **the community for AI work**. This code applies to GitHub, happytasking.com, and any other space the maintainers run in the project’s name.

We expect professionalism. Contributors are often under client NDAs and platform contracts. Protecting that boundary is part of being a good community member.

> **Share your experience, not confidential work.**

---

## Our standards

**We welcome**

- Structured, good-faith reports about pay, availability, stability, fairness, and support
- Evidence-based disagreement about methodology (TaskScore, TaskPulse, TaskRate, TaskMatch)
- Accessibility, privacy, and security improvements
- Corrections to public company or opportunity data
- Mentorship that does not demand private task material as “proof”

**We do not tolerate**

- Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination based on identity, nationality, language, or disability
- Doxxing, or linking a public username to a legal name, employer ID, or private contact without consent
- Posting confidential project information, task content, internal guidelines, client identities, or reviewer identities
- Threats, intimidation, or brigading a company, contributor, or maintainer
- Sexualized language or imagery, including unsolicited sexual attention
- Spam, phishing, or using community channels to recruit under false pretenses
- Attempting to buy, sell, or pressure changes to independent reputation scores
- Publicly sharing security-sensitive user, verification, or infrastructure data

Experience with a platform is on-topic. Gossip about named private individuals is not.

---

## Professional boundary for AI work

Happy Tasking discusses **platforms and working conditions**, not the confidential work product of AI training, evaluation, or annotation.

Do not paste:

- Project codenames
- Prompts, rubrics, answers, or labeled examples
- Internal guidelines
- Client or end-customer names
- Reviewer or worker identifiers
- Private Slack, Discord, or War Room messages

If you need to illustrate a bug, invent a harmless dummy prompt (`"Classify this public news headline"`) or describe the UI without the payload.

---

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers can remove comments, lock threads, edit or revert commits, and ban accounts that break this code. They will not use enforcement to settle a dispute about a company’s TaskScore. Score disputes go through documented methodology discussion, not through silencing critics.

---

## Scope

This code covers:

- This GitHub organization and repository
- Official Happy Tasking product surfaces (reviews, community, issues, TaskMatch)
- Official email aliases such as `security@happytasking.com` and `conduct@happytasking.com`

It does not police private conversations off the platform, except when someone uses those conversations to leak confidential work into Happy Tasking or to harass people here.

---

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to **`conduct@happytasking.com`**.

Report security vulnerabilities to **`security@happytasking.com`** ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), not to the conduct alias.

Include:

- Where it happened (issue URL, review, discussion, or PR)
- What was said or done
- Whether confidential or personal data is involved (describe, do not re-attach the payload)

You may request confidentiality. We will not require you to publish your legal name.

---

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will usually follow this ladder. Severe harm (doxxing, credible threats, clear NDA dumps) can skip steps.

1. **Correction** — Public or private reminder of the standard.
2. **Warning** — Further violations will cost access.
3. **Temporary ban** — No interaction with the community or product spaces we control for a stated period.
4. **Permanent ban** — No further participation.
